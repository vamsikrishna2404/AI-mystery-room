
## Optional Feature: Countdown Mode

1. Every mystery starts with a 10-minute countdown.
2. The countdown starts when the player enters the Investigation Room.
3. Display the remaining time in MM:SS format.
4. The timer decreases every second.
5. When the timer reaches zero, display a "Time's Up!" message.
6. Disable clue discovery, hint requests and accusations after timeout.
7. Starting a new mystery resets the countdown to 10 minutes.
8. Award a bonus score for successfully solving the mystery within the time limit (to be implemented later).
